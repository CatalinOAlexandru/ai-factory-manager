How it works

The AI does the setup. Cameras feed the dashboard.

The AI does the work of a controls engineer: it finds what is on your floor, what to measure and how to show it.

1

Describe your site

Type a sentence. Add a floor plan or a few photos if you like. The AI finds and names your machines.

2

Get a dashboard

The AI suggests what to track on each machine and builds the dashboard. Change it by asking in plain English.

3

Feed it with cameras

Put a small camera in front of each screen or gauge. It reads the values every few seconds.

    The camera reads every 2 secondsIt reads only the boxed values. It does this on the device.
  • 3

What it can read

One camera, many kinds of reading.

Each value gets the reader that suits it. The vision model marks the area one time, and you confirm it.

ReaderReadsExamplesStatus
Digits and textNumbers and words on screensGrowth chamber and incubator controllers, balances, pH and EC meters, temperature controllers, motor drive displays, old PCs that run instrument softwareIn the demo
DialA needle's position on a printed scaleHydraulic, pneumatic and vacuum gauges, CO₂ cylinder regulators, autoclave pressure, liquid nitrogen dewar levels, bimetal thermometersNext
CounterRolling digit wheelsWater, gas and electricity meters, hour meters, mechanical cycle countersNext
ColourA state or a colour indexStack lights and panel LEDs, chamber lights on or off, pH colour change, leaf greennessLater
LevelHow full something isSight glasses, hydroponic tanks, carboys, coolant and waste containersLater
PresenceWhether something is thereA tray on a shelf, a door or guard open, a part in a fixtureLater
MotionRunning or idleShakers, stirrers, fans, conveyors, spindlesLater
CountHow many objectsPots in a tray, parts in a binLater
AreaHow much of an area one colour coversCanopy cover per tray, spills, rustLater

Colour, level, presence, motion, count and area are simple filters on the same frames. They are indicators, not calibrated measurements, and the app labels them that way.
