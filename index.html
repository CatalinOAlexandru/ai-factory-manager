The problem

Every fault means a call to a specialist.

Most small factories have no one watching the machines all day. You find out about a problem when a part is late or a bill arrives.

Stops you don’t see

A machine stops and waits for material. Nobody writes it down, so nobody fixes the cause.

Faults found too late

An alarm stays on the screen for days. When someone sees it, the repair takes longer and costs more.

Energy you can’t explain

The bill goes up, but you can’t see which machine or which shift used the power.
How it works

Accessible to any factory.
Start cheap, grow for reliability.

Start with any cheap smart device. Add more sensors when you are ready. Example: an AC unit that cools the packing area.

Walk around the area with your phone. The app draws the floor plan. Mark the AC unit on the plan.
2Plug in

Features

It does the checking. You make the decisions.

A daily AI check of every machine

Each morning it checks all machines and areas. You get one short report: what needs action, what to watch, and what it can’t read yet.

  • Plain English. No codes, no charts to decode.
  • The right person. Maintenance gets the fault. The manager gets the summary.
  • Honest gaps. It says when it can’t read a machine.

It tells you what to connect

When it can’t read a machine, it says why. Then it gives you the simplest ways to connect it, with step-by-step instructions.

  • Clip on a power sensor. The quickest start.
  • Point a camera at the screen. It reads alarms and counters.
  • Connect the USB port. We fit it on the Custom plan.

Privacy & data

Your data belongs to you.

Here is what we collect and what we do with it, in plain words.

What we collect

Readings from the devices you connect, such as power, temperature and sound. Machine logs and screen images, if you connect them. Your name and work email.

You own it

Your factory data belongs to you. We use it to run AI Factory Manager for you: your checks, alerts and answers.

We do not sell it

We do not sell your data. We do not give it to advertisers.

You stay in control

You choose which machines and devices to connect. Unplug a device and it stops sending data.

We will publish our full privacy policy before launch.
